Animations:
    To animate CSS elements
        @keyframe myName{
            from{font-size:20px};
            to{font-size:40px};
        }
    Animation Properties:
        animation-name
        animation-duration
        animation-timing-function
        animation-delay
        animation-iteration-count
        animation-direction
    Animation shorthand: in a single line
    % in Animation:
        @keyframe{
            0%{font-size:20px};
            50%{font-size:30px};
            100%{font-size:40px};
        }
Positions:
    static: default Position(the top, right, bottom, left and z-index properties have no effect)
    relative: element is relative to itself( the top,right,botoom,left and z-index will work)
    absolute: positioned relative to its closest postioned ancestor(removed from the flow)
    fixed: positioned relative to browser(removed from flow)
    sticky: positioned based on users scroll position
